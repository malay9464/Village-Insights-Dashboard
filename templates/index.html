METHODOLOGY

How these numbers are calculated

Formulas, filter scopes, data dictionary and limitations.

POPULATION

Total population

Sum of Population_Total across the selected year, taluka and village scope. Crop is ignored for demographic totals.

VILLAGES

Distinct villages

Count of unique Village_Name values. The current workbook has one record per village in its single 2024 snapshot.

LITERACY

Gender gap

Arithmetic mean of each village’s male literacy rate minus female literacy rate, in percentage points. The gender chart also reports each sex’s mean rate.

HOUSEHOLD COVERAGE

Utility percentages

Arithmetic mean of village-level household electricity, water, sanitation, internet, mobile and LPG percentages. These are household coverage estimates.

FACILITIES

Facility availability

Count of village records where the binary facility flag is 1, divided by selected villages. This is a village share, not household coverage.

SOCIAL COMPOSITION

SC/ST shares & sex ratio

SC and ST population totals divided by total population. Sex ratio is total female population divided by total male population, multiplied by 1,000.

VILLAGE SIZE

Median & size bands

Median and maximum of village population. Histogram bands count villages; they do not represent the number of people in each band.

AGRICULTURE

Crop-scoped indicators

Crop selection applies to land, rainfall, employment and crop charts. Population, education, household utility and facility metrics keep the geographic scope and ignore crop.

SOURCE

ABOUT THE PROJECT

Sabarkantha District Insights

A student demonstration of data processing, comparison and accessible visualization.

PURPOSE

Explore, compare, question

Filter village-level records to inspect population, literacy, facilities, utilities, agriculture and employment.

ACCESS

Guest preview

Open the dashboard without an account. Guest sessions can read, filter and export records but cannot change account data.

GEOGRAPHY

No boundary map

The workbook includes illustrative point coordinates, not an official GeoJSON boundary set. A map would suggest more geographic accuracy than the source supports.
